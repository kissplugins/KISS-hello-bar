**KISS = Keep It Simple (Stupid)**  
Our plugins are designed to do one thing—and do it very well.

The **KISS Hello Bar** plugin (BETA) adds a simple, configurable message bar to the top and/or bottom of your website—**no upsells, no freemium features**, just clean functionality.

---

## ✨ Features

- Show a hello/message bar at the top and/or bottom of your site
- Customize message text, background color, and CTA button
- Fully responsive and admin-toolbar-aware
- Add multiple Hello Bars as a carousel.
- No bloat, no upsells — all features included

---

## 🛠️ Installation

1. **Download** the plugin ZIP from [git repository](https://github.com/kissplugins/KISS-hello-bar).
2. In your WordPress Admin, go to **Plugins > Add New**.
3. Click **Upload Plugin**, choose the zip file, and click **Install Now**.
4. Once installed, click **Activate Plugin**.

---

## ⚙️ Configuration

Go to **Settings > Hello Bar** in your WordPress dashboard to configure the plugin.

### Display Options

- **Display Hello Bar at Top of Pages**  
  Show the message bar at the top of your site.

- **Display Also on Footer**  
  Optionally display it at the bottom as well.

### Content Options

- **Message**  
  Text displayed inside the hello bar. Default:  
  `"This is your KISS Hello Bar Plugin message."`

- **Message Bar Background Color**  
  Use the color picker to customize background.

- **CTA Button Label**  
  Label for your call-to-action button (e.g., "Learn More").

- **CTA Button Link**  
  Destination URL when the button is clicked.

- **CTA Button Color**  
  Customize the button background color.

After adjusting settings, click **Save Changes** to apply them.

---

## 👀 Viewing & Troubleshooting

- Visit your site to see the bar based on your display settings.
- If updates don’t appear:
  - Clear browser and server caches.
  - Try **Incognito/Private Mode**.
- The bar auto-adjusts for the WordPress Admin Toolbar.

---

## 🚫 Disabling the Hello Bar

To turn off the hello bar:
- Uncheck both **Display Hello Bar at Top of Pages** and **Display Also on Footer**, then **Save Changes**.

---

## 🧪 Notes

- Changes apply site-wide instantly after saving.
- Compatible with all frontend pages.
- If needed, **deactivate and reactivate** the plugin to reset settings.
